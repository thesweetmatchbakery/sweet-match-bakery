How to Play
🔄
Swap to match. Tap a treat, then tap a neighbor to swap them. Line up 3 or more of the same kind in a row or column to clear them.
⭐
Score points. Every cleared treat scores. Cascades (treats falling into new matches) score extra automatically.
✨
Make a color bomb. Match 5 or more of the same treat to create a sparkling color bomb. Swap it with any treat to clear every treat of that color.
🎯
Three goal types:
⭐ Score — reach a target before running out of moves
🟦 Jelly — clear all blue jelly squares by matching on top of them
📦 Collect — gather a set number of specific treats
🍰
Build your bakery. Each level you finish unlocks a new treat on your bakery shelf.
